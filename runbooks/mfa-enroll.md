# MFA — enrol or re-enrol your authenticator

1. Go to the Self Service portal and open "Security → Multi-factor". Sign in with your SSO credentials.
2. Choose "Add authenticator" and pick the mobile app (recommended) or a hardware key.
3. On your phone, install the Firm Authenticator app from your app store, open it, and scan the QR code shown in the portal.
4. Enter the 6-digit code the app displays to confirm the pairing. Save the backup codes it offers somewhere safe.
5. Replacing a lost or wiped phone: from a trusted device, use "Remove old device" first, then repeat steps 2–4. If you cannot sign in at all, you need a help-desk reset.
6. Still stuck (no trusted device, codes rejected): escalate with your username and what step failed — never share a code with anyone, including support.
