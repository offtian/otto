# Account locked — get back in

1. First work out which lock you have: "account locked" after bad passwords is temporary; "password expired" needs a reset (use the password-reset runbook instead).
2. Temporary lock from too many attempts: wait 15 minutes without trying, then sign in once, carefully. Repeated wrong attempts restart the timer.
3. Still locked after 15 minutes, or you do not know your password: go to the Self Service portal's "Unlock / reset" flow and verify with your enrolled MFA.
4. MFA also unavailable (lost phone and no backup codes): this cannot be self-served — you need an identity-verified help-desk reset, which confirms who you are before unlocking.
5. A lock that returns within minutes of unlocking usually means a saved password somewhere is still trying the old one — check Wi-Fi, mobile mail, and mapped drives after any password change.
6. Escalate when you need the help-desk reset (step 4) or the lock keeps recurring after you have cleared saved passwords — include your username and when it started.
