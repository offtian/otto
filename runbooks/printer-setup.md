# Printing — add a network printer

1. Make sure you are on the office network (Firm-Corp Wi-Fi or a desk ethernet cable) or connected to the VPN — printers are not reachable from Firm-Guest.
2. Open the Self Service portal and run "Install Printers". It adds the follow-me queue `Firm-SecurePrint` to your device.
3. Print to `Firm-SecurePrint` from any app. The job holds on the server, not at one machine.
4. Release the job at any printer: tap your badge on the reader, or sign in on the panel with your SSO credentials, then select your job.
5. Job not appearing at the printer: confirm you printed to `Firm-SecurePrint` (not a saved direct printer), and that you badged in with the same account you printed from.
6. Printer shows an error or is offline: note the printer's asset tag (on its label) and escalate with the tag and the error on the panel.
