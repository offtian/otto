# Wi-Fi — connect to the office network

1. Choose the `Firm-Corp` network (not `Firm-Guest`). Guest is internet-only and cannot reach internal systems.
2. When prompted, sign in with your SSO username and password — Firm-Corp uses your account, not a shared Wi-Fi password.
3. Accept the certificate prompt if your device shows one; the certificate name should read `radius.firm.internal`. Do not accept a certificate with any other name.
4. If it keeps asking for credentials: forget the network, toggle Wi-Fi off and on, then rejoin — a stale saved password is the usual cause after a password change.
5. Connected but no internal access: confirm you are on Firm-Corp and not Firm-Guest, and that any personal VPN is off (it can shadow the corporate route).
6. Still no connection: note your device, location/floor, and the network name, then escalate.
